# Download Lemonomics

[Download the updated game ZIP](https://github.com/kpmgdj5cdg-creator/test-lemonomics/raw/refs/heads/main/downloads/Lemonade-Lane-Offline.zip)

1. Download and extract **Lemonade-Lane-Offline.zip**.
2. Open the extracted **Lemonade-Lane.html** in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox. No installation is required.
3. To keep existing saves, replace your old HTML at the same file path and use the same browser. Keep browser storage enabled. Your original business appears as **Player** in the first profile slot.

You can also download [the standalone HTML](Lemonade-Lane.html) using GitHub’s **Download raw file** button.

The new **Lemonomics** title screen has three separate player profiles. Choose **New game**, enter a name (up to 16 letters), and choose a male or female avatar. Saved slots display the avatar, name, game day and last-save date/time. The exit icon at the upper right opens **Save**, **Save & Quit**, and **Back to Game**. You can save partway through a selling day and resume without paying for supplies or a permit twice.

Profiles are saved in this browser for testing; they do not yet sync between PCs. Saving and starting a new profile check the pricing service when it is available. An offline HTML copy retains the last available prices.

The October 21, 2026 release expands the Neighborhood to 23 colorful modern homes along both sides of the streets, with deeper backyards, extended sidewalks and room to walk and zoom out. Backyards vary between seating, round-canopy trees, trees with circular patio tables, and flower gardens/open lawn. It also implements the playability change list: Maple Market resales, Downtown recipe books and purchased-recipe visitor bonuses, decimal recipe controls, $6 Park permits, Excel financial statements, daily results and projections, investment-secured loans, early payoff, emergency credit and business closure. All four voxel locations and avatars are retained. New businesses start with $0, no supplies and a zero recipe; use Bank → Loans, withdraw funds, choose a recipe and buy supplies.

The file contains October 19, 2026 closing prices for DIA, SPY, ARKK, SGOV, GLD and SLV, plus five years of history. Offline copies cannot automatically fetch new daily prices. History remains viewable, but investment trading and collateral sales pause when prices are stale. Daily automatic pricing requires running the editable project and its pricing service. This download is not a public hosted game.

The editable source, setup instructions and automated checks are included in the repository; see the [project README](../README.md).
